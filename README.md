# Brain Dump

**Empty your head. It sorts itself into today, later and never.**

> #17 of 17 · Wave 1: Daily keepers · Used: Daily · Easy · Revenue potential: Medium · Wellbeing & Productivity
> **Phase one**

## What it is

Type, talk or scribble everything rattling around your head, then sort it in seconds into Today, Later and Never. No projects, no tags, no setup. Built with ADHD brains in mind. Free gets 5 pages; Premium adds a whiteboard on tablets for visual thinkers. Starring Kaos doing 99 things at once at frantic genius-inventor speed, brain scrambled.

## Why it's in the portfolio

ADHD brain-dump organisers came up repeatedly in the demand research. Most to-do apps punish overwhelm with structure; a dump-first, sort-second flow matches how scattered thinking actually works.

## Who it's for

People with ADHD or busy minds who feel overwhelmed by to-do apps.

**The problem:** Too many thoughts, not enough structure, and every productivity app wants you to organise before you've even emptied your head.

## Version one

- One big dump page: type a line per thought
- Swipe or tap to sort each thought into Today, Later or Never
- Today list with gentle ticks; Never is a guilt-free bin
- 5 free pages (Work, Home, Ideas…); unlimited with Premium
- Tablet whiteboard for visual thinkers (Premium)
- Learning cards on focus, overwhelm and ADHD-friendly tricks

**Example:** At 11pm, a user dumps 23 thoughts in two minutes, sorts them in one more: 4 for Today, 9 for Later, 10 binned. They sleep.

## Later

- Voice dump
- Widgets with Kaos
- Daily 'empty your head' ritual reminder

## Animation and tone: five signature animations

**Playful.** Celebrations, confetti, cute characters, satisfying sounds and mild comic reactions. Nothing tragic.

- Dumping: Kaos juggles 99 things at genius-inventor speed
- Sorted: Kaos's scrambled brain untangles into three neat piles
- Never: thoughts get booted into a bin with a satisfying thunk
- Today done: Kaos flops onto a beanbag
- Big dump (50+ thoughts): Kaos's head steams, then a tiny 'ding'

**Line we don't cross:** Never shame a long Later list or an unfinished Today.

## AI

**Optional later.** Maybe later: suggest Today/Later/Never. Plain sorting works without it.

## Watch-outs

Never claim to treat ADHD; tips are general and sourced (NHS, ADHD UK). Keep the flow fast: the whole point is no friction.
